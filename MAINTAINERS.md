## How a decision gets made

Most decisions are made in the pull request that raises them. Where people disagree about a
detection rule, the disagreement is settled by measurement rather than by argument: run the rule
over the corpora, adjudicate the findings by hand, and the posting policy decides. That is the
whole reason the policy exists, and it applies to the maintainer's own rules as much as anyone's.

Where a decision cannot be settled by measurement, the maintainer decides and records the reasoning
in the pull request or under `docs/`. If you think a recorded decision is wrong, open an issue that
quotes it; a decision with its reasoning written down is one you can argue with.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no ladder to climb and no minimum number of merged pull requests. What earns it is a
pattern: changes that come with evidence, review comments on other people's work that catch real
problems, and judgement about what not to build. If that describes your last few months here, say
so in an issue and the maintainer will answer within a week.

## If the maintainer disappears

The licence is Apache 2.0 and every measurement, decision and runbook is in the repository rather
than in someone's head. A fork can be taken and continued by anyone, and nothing here requires
access to the hosted deployment. The hosted service at mitig8it.com is operated by the maintainer
and is not part of what a fork inherits.
